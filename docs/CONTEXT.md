# Context

This page lists the public statements that motivated Sikka. It is the only place in the repository where real entities are named, and only as the source of those statements. Sikka itself has no connection with, and no endorsement from, any entity named here.

## Public statements

All three are secondary sources (press reports). They are paraphrased briefly and have not been independently verified.

1. **Federal roads session, 22 April 2026.** A Federal National Council session on federal roads raised congestion, coordination gaps between federal and local bodies, the absence of a standard framework for intelligent transport systems, and delayed executive regulations. Source: [Gulf Today](https://www.gulftoday.ae/news/2026/04/22/dhs170-billion-allocated-for-road-projects-in-uae-minister-informs-fnc).
2. **Crashes on federal roads, 2021 to 2024.** A report relayed in the press counted 3,997 crashes on federal roads over 2021 to 2024 and listed data sharing between entities, uneven technical readiness and training among the challenges. The figure is as reported and has not been verified here. Source: [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/amp/story/uae%2Ftransport%2Fnearly-4000-uae-road-accidents-linked-to-four-risky-driving-behaviours-1.500541438).
3. **Monitoring stations.** Press reports describe 50 AI supported monitoring stations to be linked by a unified traffic platform, without rollout dates. Source: [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/amp/story/uae%2Ftransport%2Fuae-deploys-50-ai-powered-traffic-monitoring-stations-across-federal-roads-1.500543433).

## How Sikka relates to these statements

- The statements describe a coordination and data sharing problem between several entities that hold road, crash and traffic data. Sikka explores, on synthetic data, what an open data contract, conformance checks and an uncertainty aware ranking could look like for such a setting.
- The scale of the synthetic world (a few thousand crashes over five years on a network of 240 invented segments) is an assumption chosen to be plausible in order of magnitude. It is not a replication of the figure in statement 2 or of any other figure.
- Producer names in Sikka (Producer A to D) and corridor names (Dune, Pearl, Palm, Oasis, Falcon and Coral Corridor) are invented. They do not correspond to any entity or road.
- Sikka is a proposal seed prepared by a student. It is not a deployment.
